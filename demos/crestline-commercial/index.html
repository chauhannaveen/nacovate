The building

A six-storey brick-and-glass building put up in 1998 and taken back to the slab in 2023 — new lifts, new mechanicals, new lobby, and fibre from two carriers entering on opposite sides of the site. The sort of renovation that does not photograph dramatically but decides whether your team can actually work on a Monday morning.

What comes with the lease

Fibre to the suite

Two carriers in the building, diverse entry. Suites deliver with fibre already terminated.

Conference centre

Three bookable rooms seating 8, 20 and 60. Included in base rent, booked from your phone.

Secure bike room

Ninety racks, showers and lockers on the lower level. Badge access, covered by the fob you already carry.

Loading and freight

Two covered docks and a dedicated freight lift. Move-in outside business hours at no extra charge.
