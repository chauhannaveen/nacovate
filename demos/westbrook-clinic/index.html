New patient? Do the paperwork in four minutes, at home.

The clipboard in the waiting room is the worst part of a first visit, for the patient and for the staff who have to type it all in afterwards. Ours is four short steps, saved as you go, and it reaches the clinic before you do.

  1. 1Who you areName, date of birth, contact details, and who to call in an emergency.
  2. 2Your historyCurrent medications, allergies, conditions and past surgeries.
  3. 3InsurancePlan, member ID and a photo of the card — uploaded, not re-typed at the desk.
  4. 4ConsentPrivacy notice and treatment consent, signed on your phone before you arrive.
