About this house

A single-storey build on one of the quieter corners of Rowan Hill, where the lots widen out and the oaks are old enough to shade the whole back garden by mid-afternoon. The current owners took the kitchen back to brick in 2022 and left everything else alone, which turns out to have been the right call — the floors, the fireplace and the deep window reveals are all original.

Four bedrooms, three bathrooms, and a garage that a pair of cars fits into without anyone having to climb over a bonnet. The elementary school is a seven-minute walk through the neighbourhood rather than along the main road.
