Nacovate — palette options

The same block of the homepage, rendered in four palettes, light and dark. Nothing here is committed to the live site — this is a working file for picking a direction, like logo-concepts.html. Look at these on a phone too.

A · Teal on cool grey

Former — until Oct 2026 #0B7A83

What the site runs now. Reads clean and trustworthy, passes contrast comfortably. Two problems: it is the single most common accent in IT services, and it sits 7.6° of hue from the Westbrook clinic demo and 19.5° from Shanti Dental — so the brand wears the same colour as the clients it is pitching to.

B · Ink & copper

Shipped #A65B23

Warm off-white instead of cool grey, near-black with a brown undertone, and a copper accent. Copper is rare in IT services, warm enough not to feel corporate, and it clashes with none of the eight demos. The warm paper tone is most of what reads as "premium" here — cool grey is the colour of a 2014 corporate template.

C · Forest & bone

#1F4D3D

Deep green on a bone background. The most obviously expensive of the four and the most formal — it reads law firm, wealth management, consultancy. Strong if you want to look senior and are willing to look a little serious. Possibly too buttoned-up for a workshop owner in Faridabad.

D · Graphite & terracotta

#B23F2C

Near-neutral base with a burnt red accent. The most confident and the most editorial — it looks like a design studio rather than an IT firm. Highest risk: red carries urgency and error signalling, so it needs discipline, and it is one hue family away from the Everest school demo.
