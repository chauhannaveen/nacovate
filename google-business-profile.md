# Google Business Profile — setup guide & paste-ready copy

Everything needed to create the Nacovate profile at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com). Fill the form using the answers below; the long-form copy is written to paste directly.

---

## Before you start

**Sign in with `team@nacovate.com`** (the Google Workspace account), not a personal Gmail. The account that creates the profile owns it — using the business account means ownership stays with the business and you can add the others as managers later.

---

## Step 1 — Create the profile

| Field | What to enter | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Business name | `Nacovate` | Exactly this. Do **not** write "Nacovate IT Support & Web Design" — adding keywords to the name violates Google's guidelines and is a common cause of suspension. |
| Primary category | `Website designer` | Carries the most ranking weight, and it's the search with the clearest buying intent for what you actually sell. |
| Additional categories | `Software company`, `Website hosting service` | Widen the searches you appear in at no cost. Add hosting only if you'll offer it. |
| Do you have a location customers can visit? | **No** | Unless you have a real office with walk-in visitors. |
| Service area | Your city + the specific areas you'd travel to | Keep it to places you'd genuinely serve. Max 20 areas. A realistic list beats a huge radius. |
| Phone | `+91 96503 13207` | Same number as the website — consistency matters for local ranking. |
| Website | `https://www.nacovate.com/?utm_source=gbp` | The `?utm_source=gbp` tag lets you see in analytics how many visitors came from Google Business. |

> **Important:** choosing "No" for a visitable location makes this a *service-area business* and keeps your address private. If you enter a home address without hiding it, Google publishes it.

> **Avoid hardware categories.** Do not pick `Computer support and services`, `Computer repair service`, or anything with "repair" in the name. Nacovate is software — websites, automation, AI and the systems a business runs on — and those categories pull in calls about broken laptops, printers and antivirus, which wastes your time and trains Google to show you for the wrong searches. When browsing the category list, search "website", "software" and "hosting" rather than "computer".

## Step 2 — Business description

750 character limit. Paste this in (check the counter — trim the last line first if needed):

```
Nacovate is a hands-on software and automation partner for small businesses that don't have an in-house tech team.

We build clean, professional websites, automate the repetitive work that eats into your team's day — reminders, follow-ups, intake forms and reporting — and look after the software your business runs on, from email to everyday tools. We also use AI where it genuinely saves time, never as a buzzword.

We work with clinics, real-estate agencies, fabrication shops and professional services firms. You deal directly with the senior people doing the work — no call centre, no jargon.

Most clients start with one small, fixed-scope project so they can see real value before committing to anything ongoing. No long contracts.
```

> Google strips or rejects descriptions containing URLs and phone numbers — that's why neither appears above. Your contact details go in their own fields.

## Step 3 — Hours

Set realistic hours (e.g. Mon–Sat, 9:30am–7:00pm). Don't claim 24/7 unless you'll answer at 2am — "open now" drives calls, and an unanswered call is worse than being shown as closed.

## Step 5 — Verification

Google picks the method — usually **video verification** for service-area businesses, sometimes phone or postcard.

For a video call/recording, have ready:
- Something showing the business is real: laptop with the Nacovate site open, invoices, visiting cards, any branded material
- Your work area / where you operate from
- ID if asked

Verification can take a few days. The profile stays hidden until it passes.

---

## Step 6 — Photos (do not skip)

Profiles with photos get significantly more calls and clicks. Aim for **10+ real photos** — no stock images, Google can detect and penalise them.

- Logo (once chosen — see `design/logo-concepts.html`)
- You and the team at work
- Workspace / desk setup
- Screenshots of websites you've built
- An automation or dashboard in action (blur anything client-identifying)
- Any branded material (cards, invoices)

## After verification

### Reviews — the single biggest lever
Reviews drive both ranking and trust. Ask every client the moment a project lands well.

Get your review link from the profile dashboard (**Ask for reviews** → copy link), then send:

**WhatsApp, right after a project ends:**
```
Hi [Name] — glad the [website / automation] is working well for you.

If you have a minute, a short Google review would really help other business owners find us: [review link]

Thanks a lot!
```

**Gentle follow-up, a few days later:**
```
Hi [Name], just floating this up in case it got buried — a one-line review would mean a lot. No worries at all if you're busy: [review link]
```

Reply to every review, positive or negative. Public, calm replies to criticism build more trust than a wall of five stars.

### Q&A — seed it yourself
Google lets the owner post and answer questions. Add these so the answers are yours rather than a stranger's guess:

- *Do you work with businesses outside [city]?*
- *Do you take one-time projects, or only ongoing contracts?*
- *How quickly can a small business website go live?*
- *Do you provide support after the project is finished?*
- *Do you repair computers or set up hardware?* — answer plainly: no, Nacovate works on the software side (websites, automation, business systems). Answering this yourself saves you the wrong phone calls.

### Posts — once a week, 5 minutes
Short updates keep the profile active and give returning searchers something new: a project you finished, a tip ("three signs your clinic's website is losing you enquiries"), or an offer.

### Messaging
Only enable Google's chat if you'll check it daily — a slow reply is visible to everyone. WhatsApp is your stronger channel; it's fine to leave Google messaging off.

## Common mistakes that cause suspension

- Keywords stuffed into the business name
- A home address left visible instead of set as a service area
- Fake or incentivised reviews (also a legal risk in India and the US)
- Business name / phone / address that don't match the website exactly

## Later

The same information powers other listings — JustDial, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect. Keep the name, phone and website **identical** everywhere; consistency across directories is itself a local ranking factor.
